# Customers

*As of 2026-10-21.* **Everything on this page is inferred** from the product and its
[public project page](https://github.com/nsid32/inposter); no customer research exists yet. The
owner should confirm who comes first.

## Who comes first

The owner, posting on their own LinkedIn profile. InPoster was built as a personal tool (one user,
one computer, one LinkedIn profile), and every feature so far answers that person's needs. Open
source users come second: the project was polished for people cloning it from GitHub (a clear
setup script, a step-by-step Make.com guide with screenshots), but nothing is built for them that
the owner does not also use.

## Personas

### The regular poster (the owner)

A professional who wants to post on LinkedIn regularly to build their reputation in a few areas of
interest. They know what they think but writing a polished post takes too long, and they get stuck
for topics. They want two or three ready versions to choose from, a picture that fits, and a quick
look back at what they already posted so they do not repeat themselves and can continue a thread.
They care that their drafts and keys stay on their own computer.

What gets in their way today: writing from a blank page; switching between an AI chat, a photo
site and LinkedIn; remembering what they already posted.

What they use instead of InPoster: an AI chat assistant plus copying and pasting into LinkedIn, or
a hosted LinkedIn writing tool.

### The self-hosting professional

Someone who finds InPoster on GitHub: a developer, product person or consultant who posts on
LinkedIn and prefers tools they run themselves and pay for only by use. They are comfortable
installing an app from the command line and creating accounts with Anthropic and Make.com, but
they do not want to edit settings files or read code to get it working.

What gets in their way today: setup. They need an Anthropic key and a working Make.com scenario
before the first post goes out, and a mistake in the Make.com setup only shows when publishing
fails.

What they use instead: the same as the regular poster, or nothing.

## Who it is not for

*(Inferred from the project's own notes, which say it is a personal, local tool with no hosted
version, no sign-in and no multiple users.)*

- Teams and agencies managing several people's or companies' LinkedIn presence.
- People who want a hosted service they can use from any device without installing anything.
- People who need scheduled posting, analytics on how posts performed, or other social networks.
- People unwilling to create their own Anthropic and Make.com accounts.
