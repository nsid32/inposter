# Competitors

*As of 2026-10-21. Every price and claim carries its source.* No competitor research has been done
yet, and the owner did not name competitors. This page lists the **kinds** of alternatives a
user has, **inferred** from what InPoster does, with example names to research. No prices or
feature claims are given, because none have been checked; each "to research" cell needs a
dated source before it is relied on.

## The landscape

| Competitor | Who it serves | Price | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| General AI chat assistants (for example Claude or ChatGPT used directly) | Anyone writing anything | To research | Flexible, already familiar to most users | *(Inferred)* No drafts queue, no pictures in the same place, no publishing; the user copies and pastes |
| Hosted LinkedIn writing and scheduling tools (examples to research: Taplio, AuthoredUp) | People and creators growing on LinkedIn | To research | To research | *(Inferred)* Hosted: drafts and account access sit with the provider; subscription |
| General social media schedulers (examples to research: Buffer, Hootsuite) | Small businesses and teams posting to several networks | To research | To research | *(Inferred)* Built for scheduling across networks rather than for writing |
| Writing directly in LinkedIn | Everyone on LinkedIn | Free | No setup | No AI help, no drafts queue of your own |

## Where we are different

*(Inferred from the [public project page](https://github.com/nsid32/inposter).)*

- **Runs on your own computer.** Drafts, pictures and keys never leave it except in the requests
  you trigger.
- **No subscription.** You pay Anthropic, OpenAI and Unsplash directly for what you use, and
  Make.com for your scenario.
- **Writing, pictures, approval and publishing in one place**, with ideas drawn from your own
  areas of interest and your recent posts.
- **Open source** (MIT licence).

## Threats

*(Inferred.)*

- Setup effort: needing your own Anthropic key and a Make.com scenario is a real barrier next to a
  hosted tool you sign up for in a minute.
- Publishing depends on Make.com and its LinkedIn connection; a change there stops publishing.
- LinkedIn itself adding AI writing help inside the post editor would cover the simplest use.
